Reading Peeps

Privacy, in plain words

Last updated November 11, 2026

Reading Peeps is a free website that handles book club logistics. It is run by one person, Ajay Nair, a reader in Bothell, Washington. This page says what data the site keeps and what it does with it. If anything here is unclear, write to Ajay.r.nair@gmail.com and ask.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your name and email address. Passwords are stored scrambled, never in plain text. If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your name and email address.
  • Your club activity. The clubs you join, the books your club adds, votes, reading progress, ratings, the days you mark as free, discussion questions, and reactions. This is the work of the site, and the other members of your clubs can see it.
  • Notifications. A record of the notifications and emails the site sends you, like a poll opening or a meeting being set, so they can be shown in the app and marked as read.
  • Usage counts. Which pages and features get used. We use this to see what works and what is broken.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never show ads, and no advertiser or data broker gets access to your data.
  • We never track you on other websites or in other apps.
  • We never take payments. Reading Peeps is free, and it will stay free.

Who can see what

  • Members of your clubs see your name and your activity inside the club. They do not see your email address.
  • Private clubs can only be seen by their members. Public clubs can be found in search by other members.
  • A few services run the site for us: one hosts the website, one stores the data, and one sends the email. They handle data only so Reading Peeps can work.

Staying signed in

The site stores a sign in token in your browser, on your device, so you do not have to sign in every time. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

  • Leave any club whenever you like.
  • Delete your account at any time from the Me tab. That deletes your account and the personal data tied to it.
  • Or write to Ajay.r.nair@gmail.com and ask for your data to be removed by hand.

Children

Reading Peeps is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this page

If this page changes, the new version will be posted here, with a new date at the top.

Questions
