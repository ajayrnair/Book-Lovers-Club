Support

Reading Peeps is run by one person, and the fastest way to get help is email: Ajay.r.nair@gmail.com. Please write from the email address you used to sign up, and say which club you mean.

I was invited to a club. How do I join?

Open the invite link or the invite email, and sign in with the same email address the invite was sent to. An invite can be used once, and only by that email address.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Me tab and choose Delete account. That deletes your account and the personal data tied to it. If you cannot sign in, write to me and I will remove it by hand.

How do I delete a club?

Club admins can delete a club from the club settings. It asks for confirmation first.

I forgot my password.

There is no automatic reset yet. Write to Ajay.r.nair@gmail.com from your account email and I will help you get back in.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and no payments, and it will stay that way.

Where does the discussion happen?

In the room. Reading Peeps deliberately has no chat and no discussion feed. It handles the logistics so your meeting can be the conversation.
